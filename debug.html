Fish on the grid

One species from its side, procedural body or 3D model, over the fish frame's grid. Sliders move gaze, mouth, gills, turn bend, tail effort and swimming; tick auto to let a slider swing between its ends by itself.


Open the fish on the grid

Fish maker

A fish made of data: drag its outline and fins over a photograph of the real fish, paint its strains layer by layer, then Build, and Blender makes the model, shown below the drawing. Needs Blender (set BLENDER if it is not where installers put it).

Open the fish maker

Keys in the aquarium

A Activity overlay: what every fish is doing, its spells, the finger
C Collision shapes of the scenery (with the overlay on)
H Control panel: effects, fish, scenery, fps
E Scene editor
T Z P Torch, lens, pointing
Space M F Feed, sound, full screen
