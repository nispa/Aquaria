1. Reading the scene The tank, the species and the plants are described in JSON files.
  2. Building the scenery Rocks, corals and plants are grown from their recipes.
  3. Loading the fish 3D models and textures are downloaded and made ready to swim.
  4. Compiling shaders The graphics card turns water, light and skin into programs of its own.
  5. Drawing the first frame Everything comes together for the first time.

Then the effects are fitted to this screen while the fish already swim.
